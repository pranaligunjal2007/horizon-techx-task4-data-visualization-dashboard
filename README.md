📊 Project Overview

This project is completed as part of the Horizon TechX Internship – Task 4: Data Visualization Dashboard.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful visualizations and business insights. The project analyzes sales performance across product lines, branches, customer types, payment methods, and sales trends.

🎯 Objectives

- Convert raw data into meaningful charts and visualizations
- Analyze sales performance and customer behavior
- Identify high-performing and low-performing categories
- Create a dashboard for easy interpretation of business data
- Present insights that can support data-driven decision-making

🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

📁 Dataset

The project uses a supermarket sales dataset containing 1,000 sales records and 17 attributes.

The dataset includes information such as:

- Branch and City
- Customer Type
- Gender
- Product Line
- Unit Price
- Quantity
- Total Sales
- Payment Method
- Date and Time
- Customer Rating
- Gross Income

📈 Visualizations

The project includes several visualizations:

1. Sales by Product Line
2. Sales Trend Over Time
3. Sales Distribution by Product Line
4. Quantity vs Total Sales
5. Sales by Branch
6. Sales by Customer Type
7. Sales by Payment Method
8. Combined Sales Dashboard

💡 Key Insights

The analysis identifies:

- Best and lowest-performing product lines
- Highest and lowest-performing branches
- Sales contribution by customer type
- Most frequently used payment method
- Overall sales patterns and trends

📌 Business Recommendations

- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing product lines.
- Investigate lower-performing product lines for improvement opportunities.
- Study successful branch strategies and apply them to other branches.
- Strengthen customer engagement and loyalty programs.
- Continue supporting popular payment methods for customer convenience.

🏁 Key Outcome

The project successfully transforms raw sales data into clear and meaningful visual insights. The dashboard makes it easier to understand business performance and supports data-driven decision-making.
